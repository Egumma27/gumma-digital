How it works

A process without the mystery

You always know what's being built, what it costs, and when it lands. Fixed scope, fixed price, weekly demos.

01

Discovery call

Thirty minutes, free. We work out what you're really trying to solve and whether building software is even the right answer.

02

Proposal & scope

Why Gumma Digital

What working together actually looks like

You talk to the person building it

No project manager relaying your requirements to an offshore team. The person on the call is the person writing the code, so nothing gets lost in translation and decisions happen in minutes rather than sprints.

You own everything

Code in your repository, hosting in your accounts, domains in your name. If you hire someone else next year, they inherit a clean, documented codebase instead of a proprietary black box.

Fixed price, not a meter

Scope is agreed up front and quoted as one number. You can ask a question or request a tweak without watching an hourly counter tick over.

Built to be handed over

Tests where they earn their keep, sensible structure, and a README that explains how to run and deploy it. The goal is software that outlives the engagement.
